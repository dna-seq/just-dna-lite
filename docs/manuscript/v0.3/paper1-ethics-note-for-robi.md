# Note for Robi — why I need a letter from the IBAR ethics committee, and what it should say

Robi, this is the short version of what I am asking for and why, so you can raise it with the
committee without reading the whole brief.

## Why

My Rostock co-supervisor (Georg Fuellen) wants an ethics position on file for the Just-DNA-Lite
paper. German medical faculties treat anything with a human genome in it as needing a paper trail,
even when no approval is needed, and the doctoral panel will ask for it. I know the committee will
see that there is nothing to approve here. The letter saying exactly that is what I need.

## What the paper is

Open-source software that annotates a genome file on the user's own computer. The paper measures
speed and checks that our polygenic-score calculation agrees with PLINK2. The only human genomes in
it are mine and Livia Zaharia's. We both published them ourselves on Zenodo before this work, and
we are both authors. No participants, no samples, no cohort data, no clinical use, nothing reported
about either of us as people.

## What I am asking the committee to do

Issue a **short letter, with a reference number, stating that the work does not constitute
research on human subjects and does not require ethics approval**. The exact wording is in
Section 8 of the brief, ready to put on letterhead. Ideally the chair can sign it without convening
a full sitting, since it is a non-applicability letter rather than a review.

If the committee's rules do not allow that kind of letter, a favourable-opinion wording is also in
Section 8 as a fallback. I would rather have the first one.

## What I am *not* asking

- Not a full review of the manuscript.
- Not anything about the ROGEN cohort. It has not been sequenced. The letter explicitly says it is
  out of scope and will come separately, with its own protocol, before any sample is taken. This
  is in the committee's interest too, so that nobody later reads this letter as covering the cohort.
- Not an opinion on German law. The committee only records the facts; Rostock draws its own
  conclusion from them.

## What it costs

Reading the brief (about ten minutes), and a signature. The annexes are the manuscript, the two
Zenodo pages, the GitHub link, and the ROGEN project code. Nothing needs to be collected from Livia
or from me.

## One thing to check with the secretariat

Whether the letter needs to be in Romanian. English is fine for Rostock, but if the committee's
practice is Romanian I will translate the Section 8 wording.
